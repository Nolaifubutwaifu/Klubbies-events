## 1. The product in one paragraph

Klubbies Events is a private photo gallery for events. The organiser creates an event, gives each photographer their own upload link, and shares one link or QR code with attendees. Attendees confirm their email with a code, take a selfie if they want, and get every photo they appear in, at full quality, to download. Nothing is public. The organiser sees who joined and what was downloaded, and handles removal requests. It replaces the shared Google Drive link and the "send me the photos of me" DMs after every event.

- Website: https://events.klubbies.app (pricing, features, FAQ, sign up)
- iPhone app: "Klubbies Events", submitted to the App Store on 18 Oct 2026 for Australia and New Zealand, waiting on Apple's review. The app is the website plus a native QR scanner, Save to Photos and an offline screen. Attendees don't need it: everything works in any browser.
- Sister product: Klubbies (a club's long-term private archive, subscription) is not on the App Store yet. A club that runs several events a year is the natural upsell to it later.

## 3. How it works (true today)

**Organiser**
1. Creates an event: name, dates, venue, logo and brand colour (about ten minutes).
2. Chooses who gets in: anyone with the event link (default) or guest list only (import from Eventbrite, Humanitix, Luma or a spreadsheet: CSV, TSV, XLSX, XLS).
3. Creates albums (Keynote, Drinks, Headshots, any names) and one upload link per photographer.
4. Shares the QR code: printable A4 poster, PNG/SVG for slides, and an announcement email they can edit (on paid sizes Klubbies sends it to every guest, up to 3 times per event).
5. Sees who joined, views and downloads per album, and handles removal requests. Can see the gallery as an attendee with one tap.

**Photographer**: opens their link in any browser, no account, uploads full-resolution photos and videos straight into the right album, credited by name. Uploads resume after a dropped connection and run about 4 times faster than before (they now use the whole upload line).

**Attendee**: scans the QR code, enters name and email, types the 8-digit code from the email, sees the face notice, optionally takes a selfie, and gets "Your photos": every photo they're in, across all albums. They can download originals one by one or as a zip, save favourites (double tap to like), swipe through photos, and ask for a photo to come down (it hides at once while the organiser decides).

## 4. What you can claim

Each of these is live:

- Free for small events (up to 50 guests, 200 photos), no card needed. Bigger events pay once, no subscription.
- Face search: one selfie finds every photo you're in. Only you see your matches; nobody can search for another person, not even the organiser. Turning it off deletes the selfie, faceprint and matches.
- Private by default: every view and download is tied to a confirmed email; galleries are never public or indexed.
- Your logo and colour on every attendee screen, the join page and the poster.
- Photographer upload links, no account, credited by name.
- Full quality originals, photos and video (each video up to 500 MB).
- Guest list import, or open "anyone with the link" mode.
- Removal requests: hidden immediately, organiser decides within 7 days.
- Name check on guest-list events: someone who signs in under a name that doesn't match the list waits for the organiser to approve them.
- Gallery open to attendees for 12 months after the event on every size; organisers can keep photos another year for A$29.
- Stored in Sydney, Australia; faceprints never leave Sydney (Cloudflare delivers photo copies and may be outside Australia).
- No app needed for attendees; an iPhone app exists for those who want it (once Apple approves it).
- Uploads appear in the album as they land.

**Do not claim** (not built or not true): live slideshows, public share links, watermarking, exact-minute scheduled publishing, Android app, push notifications (built but switched off until the Apple push key is set up), photographer partner codes as a self-serve feature (see 6), unlimited anything.

## 5. Pricing

One payment per event, four sizes by guests who join (not tickets sold). Every size gets face search, branding, the share kit and a 12-month gallery; sizes differ only by guests and photos.

| Size | Guests | Photos and videos | Club price | Standard price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | up to 50 | 200 | A$0 | A$0 |
| Small | up to 150 | 1,500 | A$29 | A$49 |
| Medium | up to 400 | 4,000 | A$59 | A$79 |
| Large | up to 1,000 | 10,000 | A$119 | A$149 |
| Over 1,000 | quote | quote | quote | quote |

- **Club rate**: student clubs enter a campus club code on the Plan page: `UQCLUBS`, `QUTCLUBS`, `GRIFFITHCLUBS`. Max checks the club is real (union listing) before giving one out. The rate is fixed by the event's first payment.
- **Founding clubs offer**: the first 25 clubs get one Medium event free (worth A$59 at club rate) for events before 31 Mar 2027, then 50% off their second. Stripe code `FOUNDING25` (100% off, 25 uses). In return: a 15-minute call, a two-line testimonial, permission to name the club, and a short guest survey. Always show the real price with the discount, never just "free".
- **Upgrades** cost the difference (Small to Medium for a club: A$30), or the difference plus 25% once the event has run out of room. Discount codes don't apply to upgrades (deliberate).
- **Over the limit**: 10% extra guests are included; then a 48-hour window lets guests keep joining while the organiser upgrades; if nobody does, the newest guests are paused (not removed) until there's room.
- **Video** counts by length: 1 photo per 6 seconds (a minute = 10 photos).
- Not GST registered yet (under A$75,000 turnover).
- Value line that works: a 400-guest club ball costs A$59 at club rate, about 15 cents a guest.
- **Apple rule**: no prices, payment wording or "pay on the website" pointers inside the iPhone app or its App Store listing. Organisers pay on the website only. Marketing that points people to the app should never mention price in the same breath as the app.

## 6. Growth loop and tracking (built)

- **Credit line**: "Photos by Klubbies Events · Run your own event" appears on attendee pages, emails and the poster, on every size. Every attendee is a potential next organiser.
- **Tracked links**: add `?src=anything` to any link to events.klubbies.app (for example `?src=flyer-uq-union`, `?src=ig-bio`, `?src=email-batch-1`). It's remembered for 30 days and saved on the event that person creates. Use one tag per flyer location, post or email batch.
- **"How did you hear about us?"** on Create an event: Flyer or poster, Instagram or TikTok, Email from Klubbies, At an event (saw the credit), A photographer, Another club, Other.
- Club codes and Stripe promotion codes used at checkout are recorded too, so a photographer can be given their own Stripe code (for example `JANEPHOTO20`) and their referrals counted. The "free event per 3 referrals" reward is manual: Max checks the usage report and comps an event.
- Max has a private usage report (per event: size, price paid, guests, selfies, photos, downloads, cost, margin, source).

## 8. Channel plan (from the launch strategy, 27 Sep 2026)

Two waves: end-of-year club events this semester for real data and case studies, then a bigger push at O-Week 2027 (Feb to Mar), when new committees plan their year. December and January are quiet (and Max travels over Christmas): use them to turn first events into case studies and the O-Week kit.

| Rank | Channel | What to do | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Direct outreach to club execs | 15 to 20 personal emails and Instagram DMs a week, each naming their next event, offering the founding deal | Now to mid Nov, again Feb |
| 2 | Shoot the first events himself | Photograph 3 to 5 club events free or at cost, deliver through Klubbies Events: demo photos, case studies, testimonials | Oct to Nov |
| 3 | Photographer partners | His photography students and Brisbane event photographers: clients get 20% off with their code | From Nov |
| 4 | In-product credit | Already built (section 6) | Live |
| 5 | Student unions | Ask UQU, QUT Guild, Griffith clubs teams for a mention in exec training or newsletters; offer a free event as in-kind sponsorship | Feb to Mar 2027 |
| 6 | Short videos | Screen recordings: "find every photo of you from the ball in 10 seconds" for Instagram and TikTok | Ongoing |
| 7 | Flyers with tracked QR codes | Approved boards near club spaces and at O-Week, one `?src=` per location; "Are you in this photo?" crowd shot leading to a demo event | O-Week 2027 |
| 8 | Business events | LinkedIn, agencies, venues, using club case studies | From Q2 2027 |

**Spam Act basics** (not legal advice): email only club addresses that are publicly listed (union directory, club website) and relevant to their role, unless the listing says no marketing. Every email says who you are, gives contact details and has a working unsubscribe. Collect addresses by hand; no scraping tools or bought lists.

**Photos in marketing**: only use photos of people who agreed (Max's own shoots with releases). The App Store screenshots use demo photos.

## 9. What to measure

| Metric | First target |
| --- | --- |
| Where organisers came from | Known for every event |
| Setup completion (event reaches its first upload) | 70% |
| Guest join rate (joined ÷ expected guests) | 40% |
| Selfie rate | 40% |
| Download rate (joined guests who download at least one photo) | 60% |
| Time from event end to first published album | under 72 hours |
| Real cost per event | under 25% of price |
| Organisers who run a second event or bring another club | 1 in 3 |

After each founding event, ask: what almost stopped you using it, what would you pay for the next one, and who else should use it.
