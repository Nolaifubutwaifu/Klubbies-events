## What it is

A private photo gallery for events. The organiser creates an event, gives each photographer their own upload link, and shares one link or QR code. Attendees confirm their email with a code, take a selfie, and face search finds every photo they appear in, at full quality, to download. Nothing is public.

- Website: https://events.klubbies.app
- iPhone app "Klubbies Events": submitted to Apple on 18 Oct 2026 (Australia and New Zealand). Optional: everything works in a browser.
- Sister product: Klubbies, a club's long-term private archive on a subscription.
- Founder: Max, Brisbane, solo, evenings and weekends.

## The USP: face search, done privately

One selfie finds every photo you're in. Face search on its own is not unique (Waldo, Kwikpic and others have it). The combination is:

1. **Only you see your matches.** Nobody can search for another person, not even the organiser. Turning it off deletes the selfie, faceprint and matches.
2. **Data in Sydney**, run by an Australian operator. Faceprints never leave Sydney.
3. **The organiser's brand on every screen**, not ours.
4. **Pay once per event**, guests never pay, full-quality originals.

## Pain points it solves

1. **"Send me the photos of me"**: attendees find their own photos from one selfie instead of scrolling 2,000 or messaging the organiser.
2. **The shared Google Drive link**: public to anyone with the link, unbranded, unsearchable.
3. **Slow delivery**: photos land in the gallery as the photographer uploads them, so attendees can have them the same night.
4. **Lost quality**: attendees download the photographer's original, not a compressed copy from Instagram or WhatsApp.
5. **Privacy at work events**: only confirmed emails get in, face search is opt-in, and anyone can ask for a photo to come down (hidden at once, organiser decides within 7 days).
6. **Photographer handover**: one upload link per photographer, no account, credited by name, uploads resume after a dropped connection.
7. **No idea who saw what**: the organiser sees who joined and what was downloaded.

**The business pitch**: every attendee has their own photos before they get home, under your brand, so they share them. Pitch the outcome. There is no built-in "share to LinkedIn" button.

## What's live (safe to claim)

Free tier, face search, private by default, branding, photographer links, full-quality photos (up to 50 MB) and video (up to 200 MB per video, since 17 Nov 2026), guest list import (Eventbrite, Humanitix, Luma, spreadsheets) or open link mode, removal requests, name check on guest lists, 12-month gallery, data in Sydney, uploads appear as they land, A4 QR poster and announcement email, tracked `?src=` links, a private usage report with cost and margin per event.

**Not built, don't claim**: live slideshows, public share links, watermarking, share to LinkedIn, Android app, push notifications (built, off until the Apple push key exists), self-serve photographer partner codes, anything "unlimited". No launch outside Australia and New Zealand until face recognition is checked against GDPR and US biometric laws.
