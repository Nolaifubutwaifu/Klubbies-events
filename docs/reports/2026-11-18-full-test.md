|---|---|---|---|

## Findings

1. **Viewer and event cover load slowly on slow links** (fixed in this branch). The main photo now loads at high priority and the viewer's neighbours at low priority (`fetchPriority`).
2. **Sign-in bursts** (fixed in this branch). Retries raised from 3 to 4 (10 s worst case). Real fix stays IP address forwarding in Supabase Auth, after the season.
3. **Viewer Download on iPhone saves to Files, not Photos.** The album's "Save to Photos" already uses the share sheet; the single photo button should too. Not fixed yet.
4. **Album page server time grows with album size** under 50 page loads at once (3.0 s typical at 352 photos, 4.1 s at 2,000). Pages are already paged at 240. Worth profiling before a 3,000+ photo event.
5. **Consent checkbox:** a tap on the box itself did not tick it in the simulator; a tap on the text did. Check on a real phone.
6. **Dark event pages:** content scrolls under the status bar with no backing, so the clock overlaps text.
7. Still open from 8 Oct: a focus ring is drawn on the logo when the menu opens.
8. **Album zip is slow for many small files** (about 2 MB/s, see below). Not fixed yet.
9. **Big picks on an iPhone look frozen.** Max picked 160 photos: nothing happened for a long while, then they uploaded at about 1.4 files a second (58 files, 159 MB in 41 s, all ready). iOS converts every picked file before handing any to the page, and the page can't show progress for that step. A hint under "Add photos" on phones now says it can take a minute to start (this branch).
10. **Selection bar turns into a round blob on phones** (Max spotted it). It is a pill (`rounded-[999px]`) whose buttons wrap to a second row on a narrow screen. Now a rounded rectangle on phones, count and Done on top, actions in a full-width row below; still a pill on wider screens (this branch).

## Full album zip

Every part downloaded to the end and checked with `unzip -t`: 16 parts, 2,016 files (the 2,000 plus Max's 16), no errors. Full-size parts stop at the 500 MB cap (30 to 34 files), the rest at 150 files.

A first attempt stopped at part 7 with a read timeout, at the same time this Mac's whole internet connection slowed to 12 to 17 s per request (Google too), so that was the connection, not the server.

Speed: parts of large files run at about 15 MB/s, parts of 150 smaller files at about 2 MB/s (78 s for 150 MB). The zip reads files one after another, so per-file latency dominates. A guest downloading all 2,016 waits about 20 minutes over 16 parts. Fine for now; fetching the next few files ahead while one streams would cut it a lot.
