# Round 1: Maya, Attendee Advocate

**Verdict.** The bones are kind: the copy is mostly plain, the join screen carries the event's own letterhead, and the privacy wording is unusually honest.
But the two moments that matter most break for real people: getting in at a busy venue (a shared-IP rate limit quietly stops codes) and getting photos *onto the phone* (downloads go to Files as zips, and the one "Save to Photos" button very likely stops after 8 photos).
The photographer in the hallway on one bar has no safety net: no auto-retry, no "retry all", no leave-page warning, and a misleading "your link stopped working" after a long stall.

I walked it as a guest: QR, `/e/[handle]`, `/signin?event=`, code, event home, face notice, `/me`, album, viewer, Saved, downloads. Then as a freelance photographer on `/g/[token]` with 400 JPEGs. Then the emails. Static read only; I have not run anything on a device. Where a claim depends on browser behaviour I say so.

### M-1 Everyone on the venue Wi-Fi shares one rate limit, so after about 20 guests nobody gets a code, and the screen still says "A code is on its way"
- **Where:** `lib/auth/rate-limit.ts:6-8` (`codeRequestPerIp` 20/hour, `verifyPerIp` 60/hour), `lib/auth/request.ts:12` (the key is the first `x-forwarded-for` address), `lib/auth/flow.ts:130-134` (a request over the limit just returns, with no email sent), `app/api/auth/request_code/route.ts:17-25` (always answers OK), `app/(auth)/signin/code/page.tsx:37` ("A code is on its way to…"), `app/api/auth/verify_code/route.ts:21-23` (over the limit gives `CODE_REJECTED`). Affects attendees in the join flow, at the event itself.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #9 help users recognise and recover from errors, #1 visibility of system status, plus a bug. **4**. S.
- **Problem:** The poster QR gets scanned in the room, on the venue's Wi-Fi or on a carrier's shared address (CGNAT). Every guest behind that NAT counts against one bucket. Guest 21 within the hour gets "A code is on its way", and no code ever comes. They try "Send a new code" (see M-6), which also counts. `hitRateLimit` records the hit even when it refuses (`rate-limit.ts:29`), so a busy room keeps the bucket full for as long as people keep trying. Worse, past 60 verifications an hour from that address, a guest who types the **right** code is told "That code didn't match", which blames them for our limit. This lands exactly when the organiser is watching ("scan the QR on the screen now, everyone").
- **Fix:** Lean on the per-email limits that already exist (`codeRequestPerEmail` 5/hour, `MAX_VERIFY_ATTEMPTS` 5 per pending code) and make the per-IP limits a flood guard: about 300 to 500 an hour, or key them per IP *and* event for `flow: "join"`. Don't record a hit that was refused. When verification is limited, say so ("Too many sign-ins from this network right now. Wait a minute and try again") instead of returning `CODE_REJECTED`; the neutral answer only matters for code *requests*, not verification. Log or alert when an IP bucket trips during an event's dates.

### M-2 On a phone, "Download" puts photos in the Files app as a file or zip, never in Photos, and only one screen knows how to fix that
- **Where:** Viewer `app/(app)/e/[handle]/a/[albumId]/[mediaId]/Viewer.tsx:296-300` (`<a href=/api/media/…/download>`); Your photos `app/(app)/e/[handle]/me/page.tsx:142-144` and the home card `components/event/YourPhotosCard.tsx:37-39` (zip); Saved `app/(app)/e/[handle]/saved/SavedTabs.tsx:176-181` ("Download these", a zip); the selection bar `components/AlbumGrid.tsx:200-209` (a zip). Compare `components/AlbumActions.tsx:337-345, 464-469`: only the album header detects the share sheet and offers "Save to Photos". Affects attendees on phones (most of them) in viewer, me and saved.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #2 match between system and the real world, #4 consistency. **3**. M.
- **Problem:** On iPhone Safari, a guest taps Download, gets "Do you want to download…?", and the photo goes to Files > Downloads. On "Download all" they get a zip of up to 150 originals (possibly hundreds of MB on mobile data) in Files, where they have to unzip it and share each photo into Photos. To a guest, "download" means "it's in my camera roll". The product promises "get every photo they're in" (README line 3), and the headline screen, Your photos, is the one with no phone-friendly path. Album pages have one, so the same person sees "Save to Photos" in one place and a zip in another.
- **Fix:** Pull the share-sheet logic out of `AlbumActions` into a shared `SaveToPhone` control and use it in the viewer (one file, shared straight from the tap, which keeps the gesture alive), on `/me`, on the home card and in Saved. Keep the zip as the laptop path and the "More" option. On phones, show the size before a zip starts ("About 420 MB. Best on Wi-Fi.").

### M-3 The album's "Save to Photos" very likely stops after the first 8, and it saves smaller WebP copies and video posters, not the originals
- **Where:** `components/AlbumActions.tsx:395-438` (loops over the whole album in batches of 8, calling `navigator.share` after awaited fetches, once per batch); `app/api/media/sign/route.ts:30` (the `display` variant is `display_path`, and for a video it is the **poster**); `lib/media/prepare.ts:95` (display is WebP at about 2000px). Affects attendees on iPhone Safari on the album page.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** Bug, #1 visibility of system status, #2. **3**. M.
- **Problem:** `navigator.share` needs a fresh user tap. The first batch runs after several network fetches, which on one bar can outlast the tap. Every later batch has no tap at all, so Safari will very likely reject it, and the guest sees "Saving stopped. You can also use Download all." after 8 photos (or none). I can't run Safari here; please confirm on a device. Even when it works, the guest gets a downscaled WebP (which iOS Photos treats oddly), and a video saves as a still frame. That breaks the "full quality" promise without saying so.
- **Fix:** One tap per batch: "Save 1 to 8", then the button becomes "Save next 8 (9 to 16 of 120)", with the files prefetched before the tap. Share the original (or a full-size JPEG for HEIC and WebP) and the real video file. Default to the guest's own matches ("Save your 14 photos") before offering the whole album.

### M-4 "Download all" on Your photos quietly gives you 150 at most; the home card offers "Download (part 1)" and part 2 doesn't exist anywhere
- **Where:** `app/api/events/[id]/me/zip/route.ts:18, 28-30` (supports `?part=`, ordered by `media_id`); `app/(app)/e/[handle]/me/page.tsx:142` (no part handling, labelled "Download all"); `components/event/YourPhotosCard.tsx:38` ("Download (part 1)"). A grep for `?part`/`part=` finds only the album actions (`components/AlbumActions.tsx:73`). Error responses are raw JSON (`me/zip/route.ts:17, 21, 31, 43`; `app/api/media/[id]/download/route.ts:11, 19, 22, 24, 34`). Affects attendees with a lot of matches (speakers, award winners, organisers' own staff) on home and `/me`.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** Bug, #1, #9. **3**. S.
- **Problem:** The person most photographed (the keynote speaker, say) taps "Download all" and gets 150 photos with no hint that more exist. On the home card they're told "part 1" with no way to reach part 2. The zip order (`media_id`) also doesn't match the page order (`sort_at`), so "which 150" is effectively random. If the request fails (gallery closed, link expired, nothing downloadable), a phone shows a bare `{"error":"Nothing to download"}` page or saves a `.json` file.
- **Fix:** Reuse `AlbumActions`' parts list ("Download part 1 of 3", and so on) on both screens, and order parts by `sort_at` so they match what's on screen. Have the download and zip routes redirect to a small HTML "Couldn't download that" page with a Back link, rather than returning JSON to a browser navigation.

### M-5 Opening a photo from "Your photos" drops you into the album: swiping shows strangers, and Close takes you somewhere you never were
- **Where:** `app/(app)/e/[handle]/a/[albumId]/[mediaId]/page.tsx:66-68` (`albumHref` and `itemHrefBase` are always the album); `Viewer.tsx:136-137, 185-187, 157` (prev, next, Close and Escape all go to the album); links in from `me/page.tsx:171`, `YourPhotosCard.tsx:51`, `me/Suggestions.tsx:61`, `saved/SavedTabs.tsx:187`. Affects attendees in the me, viewer and saved flows.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #3 user control and freedom, #6 recognition rather than recall. **3**. M.
- **Problem:** I'm on "Your photos", I tap my photo, the viewer says "37 of 412 · Networking", and swiping shows 411 photos of other people. I tap X and land in the Networking album, not back on my photos. To get back I have to remember the tab bar, which is hidden in the viewer (`MemberTabBar.tsx:263, 314`). On a phone, the browser Back button is my only way home, and every swipe used `router.replace`, so Back usually works, but Close doesn't.
- **Fix:** Carry the context in the URL (`?from=me` or `?from=saved`). In that mode the viewer's sequence and filmstrip are your matches or your saved photos, the counter reads "3 of 14 photos of you", and Close returns to the screen you came from.

### M-6 "Send a new code" doesn't send a code: it dumps you on a blank form to retype your name and email
- **Where:** `app/(auth)/signin/code/CodeForm.tsx:122-124` (a link to `restartHref`); `app/(auth)/signin/code/page.tsx:17, 26-28` ("Use a different email" goes to the same place); `app/(auth)/signin/SignInForm.tsx:86, 100` (no `defaultValue`, even though the email is in the `kb_signin` cookie, `request_code/route.ts:26`). Affects attendees and organisers in the code-entry flow.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #2 (the label promises something it doesn't do), #3, #6. **3**. S.
- **Problem:** On one bar the email is slow, so the guest taps "Send a new code" and gets the whole join form again, empty, with thumbs and autocorrect in the way. Two links with different names go to the same place. With M-1, every extra round trip also uses up the shared limit.
- **Fix:** Make "Send a new code" a button that POSTs `request_code` again with the stored email (keep the name in the cookie, or in `pending_sign_ins`, which already has `claimed_name`). Show "Sent again. Check the newest email." and a 30-second cooldown ("Send again in 0:24"). Keep "Use a different email" as the way back, and prefill the email there too.

### M-7 The face notice blocks the headline feature, hides what it asks you to acknowledge, and gives an uneasy guest nothing to do except "I understand"
- **Where:** `app/(app)/e/[handle]/page.tsx:83-108` (while the notice is up, the "Find the photos you're in" card isn't rendered); `components/FaceNotice.tsx:25-35, 37-49` (details collapsed under "What this means"; the only button is the acknowledgement); `lib/faces/copy.ts:36-42` ("analysed for faces", "faceprint"); then `app/(app)/e/[handle]/me/Enrol.tsx:53-109` (about 150 words: lead, a "biometric information" paragraph, three bullets, a 26-word checkbox, and a "Find my photos" button that stays disabled with no reason given). Affects attendees in their first minute in the event.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #2 (jargon: faceprint, biometric, enrol), #10 help, trust and consent, #1 (a disabled button with no reason). **3**. M.
- **Problem:** The guest arrives wanting their photos and is met by "This event's photos are analysed for faces" and a button reading "I understand that faces in this event's photos are analysed." The facts are folded away, so they're asked to acknowledge something they haven't been shown. The notice tells them their face is faceprinted whether or not they take part (`copy.ts:38`), then offers no next step: no "what can I do about it", no link to ask for photos of them to come down, no privacy page. Those who do tap on reach a consent wall, and the button stays grey until they've *both* picked a selfie *and* ticked the box, with nothing saying so. I'm not asking to weaken the disclosure (it is deliberate and the honesty is a strength); I'm asking that it read as care, not a hurdle.
- **Fix:** One card, not two steps: a plain headline ("Find yourself in the photos, or skip it"), the three facts **visible** in short sentences, and two buttons, "Find my photos" and "Not now", both of which record the acknowledgement. Add one line for the uneasy guest: "Don't want to be in a photo? Open it and tap Request removal." and link the privacy page. In `Enrol`, put the selfie step first, keep the three bullets, shorten the checkbox to "I agree to Klubbies Events making a faceprint from my selfie to find me in this event's photos" with a "What's a faceprint?" disclosure, and show a hint under the disabled button ("Add a selfie and tick the box"). Replace "enrol" in user-facing copy (`Enrol.tsx:142` "You can enrol again later") with "You can turn it back on later."

### M-8 The photographer on one bar has no safety net: no auto-retry, no "Retry all", the failures scroll out of view, and nothing warns before leaving
- **Where:** `app/g/[token]/GuestUploader.tsx:156` ("Drop photos and videos here" on a phone), `:202` (`jobs.slice(-30)`, so earlier failures vanish), `:226` (Retry is per file only); `lib/media/guest-queue.ts:6-9` (no resumable TUS), `:145-163` (a whole-file PUT; a drop at 90% restarts from zero), `:231-233` (any failure is terminal, with no backoff and no `online` listener). No `beforeunload` on this page, while the member uploader has one (`app/(app)/UploadTray.tsx:16-21`). Affects the photographer in the upload flow, on bad signal.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #3 user control and freedom, #9, #1. **3**. M.
- **Problem:** 400 photos, hallway signal. The connection drops for 20 seconds and 60 files turn "Failed: Connection lost. Retry when you're back online." Only the last 30 rows show, so the photographer can't even see most of the failures, let alone tap Retry 60 times. If they tap Back, or the phone sleeps, everything stops without a warning. The page's advice ("open this link again and drop the same files") assumes a laptop. On a phone it means reselecting 400 photos from the camera roll by hand.
- **Fix:** Retry a network failure on its own with backoff (for example 2, 5 and 15 seconds, then wait for the `online` event). Add a "Retry 60 failed" button and a "Show failed only" filter, and always pin failed rows above the last 30. Add the same `beforeunload` guard as `UploadTray`, and request a Screen Wake Lock while busy, with a fallback line ("Keep your screen on"). Say "Tap to choose photos" on touch devices. In the longer term, chunk large files (signed-URL TUS or multipart) so one drop doesn't restart a 200 MB video.

### M-9 Retrying after a long stall says "The link stopped working. Ask the organiser for a new one", and Retry never recovers
- **Where:** `lib/media/guest-queue.ts:57` (any 403 or "expired" text maps to that message), `:177-178` (a retry reuses the cached `job.ticket`), `:99-103` (`retry` doesn't clear it), `:59` (any other error shows raw server text, cut at 160 characters). Signed upload URLs from `app/api/guest/[token]/ticket/route.ts:89-93` expire (Supabase signed upload URLs are good for 2 hours). Affects the photographer during upload, coming back later (from the hotel Wi-Fi, say).
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** Bug, #9. **3**. S.
- **Problem:** The photographer's upload stalls on the night. They tap Retry at the hotel two hours later. The cached signed URL has expired, storage says 403, and the page tells them their link is dead and to bother the organiser. The link is fine, and every further Retry fails the same way. The photographer emails the organiser at 11pm; the organiser makes a new link they didn't need.
- **Fix:** On a 400 or 403 from the storage PUT, drop `job.ticket` and request a new one. Show the "link stopped working" message only when `/api/guest/[token]/ticket` itself returns 403. Replace the raw-text fallback with "Something went wrong with this file. Tap Retry." and log the detail.

### M-10 "Save" means three different things, and the Saved page's Downloads tab promises what it can't show
- **Where:** The heart is "Save" (`Viewer.tsx:285`, `AlbumGrid.tsx:197`), and the toast says "Saved 3 to your favourites" (`AlbumGrid.tsx:190`); the phone action is "Save to Photos" (`AlbumActions.tsx:467`); the tab is "Saved", containing "Favourites" and "Downloads" (`SavedTabs.tsx:150-151`). The Saved page says "Photos you saved from this event, ready to download" and "It turns up here at full quality, ready to download" (`saved/page.tsx:71, 79`). The Downloads empty state says "Anything you save to your phone or pull down as a zip shows up here" (`SavedTabs.tsx:241-242`), but zips are logged with no `media_id` (`app/api/events/[id]/me/zip/route.ts:45`), the page keeps only rows with a `media_id` (`saved/page.tsx:32`), and "Save to Photos" isn't logged at all. Affects attendees in the viewer, album and saved flows.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #2 match with the real world, #4 consistency. **2**. S.
- **Problem:** A guest taps the heart labelled "Save" and reasonably thinks the photo is now on their phone. It isn't; it's on a list in our app. Later, the Downloads tab is empty after they downloaded a zip, which the copy just told them would show up. I know the 27 Sep page walk changed "Favourite" to "Save" on purpose, so it matched the "Saved" tab. I'm reopening that: in a photo app, "save" means camera roll.
- **Fix:** Call the heart "Favourite" and the tab "Favourites"; that list idea is familiar from every photo app. Keep "Save" for the phone ("Save to phone", or "Save to Photos" on iOS). Either log zip and share-sheet saves per item, or change the empty-state copy to "Photos you download one at a time show up here."

### M-11 No way to take back a tap on "Yes", "Not me" or "This isn't me", and the confirmation toast follows you around
- **Where:** `app/(app)/e/[handle]/me/Suggestions.tsx:69-92` (applied at once, no undo; the copy says "we never suggest that photo again", line 51); `Viewer.tsx:340-353` ("This isn't me", permanent); `app/(app)/face-actions.ts` `decideFaceMatchAction` (a reject writes a durable `face_rejections` row; a confirm promotes the face to a reference). `Viewer.tsx:133, 309-316`: `message` is never cleared, so "Thanks. We won't suggest this one again." stays on screen as you swipe through other photos. An undo already exists in the code: `components/UndoBar.tsx`. Affects attendees on `/me` and in the viewer.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #3 user control and freedom, #5 error prevention. **2**. S.
- **Problem:** A thumb slip on a small Yes/Not me pair, side by side in a 188px card, is permanent. A wrong "Yes" teaches the matcher a stranger's face, and future photos of that stranger arrive as "photos of you". A wrong "Not me" hides a real photo of you for good, with no way to find it again from `/me`.
- **Fix:** Reuse `UndoBar` for 10 seconds after each decision (delay the server write, or add a revert action). Time out the viewer toast after 3 seconds and clear it on navigation. Give the "Not me" button a little more space from "Yes".

### M-12 First-run friction on sign-in: a name you have to remember, a password option for people who never made one, and an email from a brand they've never heard of
- **Where:** `app/(auth)/signin/SignInForm.tsx:81-94` (the returning-member flow requires "Full name … As you gave it when you joined"); `:119-130` ("Use a password instead" shows on the event join screen too, `flow === "join"`); `lib/email/send.tsx` `sendSignInCode` (subject "12345678 is your Klubbies Events code"); `emails/SignInCode.tsx:9` (the preview names the vendor, not the event). Affects attendees in sign-in and join.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #6 recognition rather than recall, #2, #8 minimalism. **2**. S.
- **Problem:** A returning guest has to recall how they typed their name weeks ago, for no visible reason (it only feeds `name_mismatch`). A first-time guest sees "Use a password instead" and wonders whether they should have made one; tapping it removes the name field and shows "Log in". The code email's subject and sender are "Klubbies Events", not "Brisbane Product Summit", the only name the guest knows, so it's easy to miss in a busy inbox or take for spam.
- **Fix:** Drop the name field from the member sign-in (or make it optional). Hide the password toggle on `flow="join"`. Put the event in the subject when there is one: "12345678 is your code for Brisbane Product Summit".

### M-13 On a bad connection, several actions fail silently
- **Where:** `components/FaceNotice.tsx:42-45` (when `res.error` is set nothing is shown; the button just re-enables); `me/Suggestions.tsx:74-75, 86-87` (the card disappears optimistically, and a failed write is never reported, so it comes back next visit); `me/LookingNow.tsx:19` (errors are treated as "pending", so it polls forever with "Checking for matches"); `Viewer.tsx:291` (a failed favourite reverts with no message). Affects attendees on poor signal.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #1 visibility of system status, #9. **2**. S.
- **Problem:** On one bar, the guest taps "I understand" and nothing happens, so they tap again; the Yes/Not me answers they gave come back tomorrow. People conclude the app is broken, or that they did something wrong.
- **Fix:** One small pattern everywhere: on error, show "Couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again." next to the control, keep the optimistic state only on success, and have `LookingNow` say "Lost connection, still trying…" after two failed polls.

### M-14 The closed-gallery screen is a dead end
- **Where:** `app/(app)/e/[handle]/layout.tsx:68-74` ("If you still need a photo, contact the organiser directly."), with no contact, link or email shown. Affects attendees after the access window closes.
- **Heuristic / severity / effort:** #10 help and documentation, #3. **1**. S.
- **Problem:** "Contact them directly" assumes the guest has the organiser's details. Most attendees of a conference don't.
- **Fix:** Show the host organisation with a contact address if the organiser set one, or a "Ask the organiser" button that sends a message through `/support#contact` with the event prefilled. Also say what happened to their selfie ("Your selfie and face data were deleted when the gallery closed"); that's true (decisions 77) and it reassures people.
